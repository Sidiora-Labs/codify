# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Codify is a technical project, and we intend to keep the discussion technical. Everyone who participates, whether by opening issues, reviewing code, or asking questions, deserves to do so without harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code and ideas, not people. "This allocation leaks on the error path" is useful. "Did you even test this?" is not.
- Assume competence and good faith. Most bad patches come from missing context, not carelessness. Supply the context.
- Be direct and be specific. Vague disapproval wastes everyone's time; a concrete objection with a suggested alternative moves the work forward.
- Accept review gracefully. Requested changes are part of the process, not a judgment of you.
- Respect maintainer decisions. Disagreement is fine and can be argued once, well. Relitigating settled decisions across multiple threads is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any project space
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Sustained disruption of discussions, including bad-faith arguing and sealioning
- Any conduct that would be clearly inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication channel. It also applies when representing the project in public, for example posting from a project account or speaking about the project at an event.

## Enforcement

Report violations to conduct@sidiora.com. Reports go to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md); if your report concerns a maintainer, say so and it will be handled by the others.

All reports are reviewed, kept confidential to the extent possible, and answered within 7 days. Depending on severity and history, responses range from a private warning, to temporary restriction from project spaces, to a permanent ban. Deliberate false reports are themselves a violation.
